Agent memory usually stays a passive footnote: past traces you pull up and glance at when needed. The idea making the rounds is to stop glancing and start distilling — turn that memory into a skill, a reusable ability the agent can just call. So instead of re-reading what worked, you promote it into a capability. The catch is that the wins aren't the only thing that distills cleanly.

## What this means for ThakiCloud

ThakiCloud already runs this as a pipeline. Paxis, the agent platform, distills the patterns that keep recurring into skills, and Metis, the training-and-inference platform, wires those skills into the next job. This comic ships itself the same way, every day. We just picked a direction: rather than training your memory into someone else's model and renting it back, you stack the skills inside your own walls — on-prem, meaning the models, data, and infrastructure all run under your control. That way the experience your team accumulates compounds as your asset, not the landlord's.
