## What this means for ThakiCloud

The label changes, the work rarely does. Shaping the prompt, selecting the right context, wrapping it in a harness of tools and verification gates, feeding results back through a loop, and wiring the pieces into a graph. Paxis already runs all five layers as real skills and rules. So when the next buzzword lands, we add a layer to the same skeleton on Metis instead of rebuilding the stack. And when that skeleton sits on-prem, a few more renamings later your data and models are still yours.
