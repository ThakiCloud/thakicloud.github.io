## What this means for ThakiCloud

Once an agent actually moves a case forward, the question changes. It is no longer whether the model is clever, but whether there is a record of who clicked what, under which authority. Paxis runs work automation on top of human approval steps and an audit trail, and Signum ties every click back to an identity and a permission. Swapping in the newest model is fine. Where that model runs, and where the approval log lives, is the part worth keeping inside your own facility, which is what Aegis is for. Rent the brain when you need it. Keep the execution and the accountability at home.
