## What this means for ThakiCloud

The takeaway is that what 'open' is worth always comes down to who owns the house. ThakiCloud's answer is sovereign on-prem: model, data and infrastructure all run inside the customer's own facility, so there is nobody to sell the house to, no rent to pay, and no news that can reach you. The Paxis agent platform runs a daily swarm check on whether the stack is still open, while the Metis learning and inference platform keeps reasoning on the customer's own GPUs.
