## What this means for ThakiCloud

Summarizing well is easy next to tagging every sentence with where it came from. The Paxis research pipeline re-checks the real source behind each claim and marks or drops any line it cannot ground, which keeps plausible hallucinations out of the report. Keep the research data inside on-prem Metis and you can run the same literature synthesis without leaking unpublished results or pre-patent work. Grounded citations and data sovereignty turn out to be the same thing.
