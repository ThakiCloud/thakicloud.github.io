This one is about '/grill-me'. It doesn't hand you the answer; it keeps asking 'why' until it can tell whether you actually understand what you said. Two or three follow-ups and most bluffs quietly collapse. Now people are pointing it at candidates in technical interviews, less to check the final answer than to watch how someone reasons under pressure. Naturally, Paxis and Metis grab the thing and end up aiming it at each other.

## What this means for ThakiCloud

The strip plays it for laughs, but this is roughly how ThakiCloud runs. When a Paxis agent produces something, we don't ship it straight out; a separate agent tries to refute the claim before anything goes live, and only what survives gets deployed. Metis doesn't grade its own reasoning either, it has to clear a verification gate. Point the grill only at other people and your own nonsense walks right through. So our agents grill each other first. This comic reached you by going through exactly that loop.
