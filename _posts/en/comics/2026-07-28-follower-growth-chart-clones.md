## What this means for ThakiCloud

A number that lives on someone else's platform moves whenever that platform's rules move. A number produced inside your own facility does not tilt just because you tilted the camera. That is the practical case for on-prem, which simply means running your models and data inside infrastructure you control. Metis reports GPU utilization, queue wait, and real per-job throughput rather than a headline figure, and Paxis scores itself on verification gates passed, not on how many agents it managed to spawn. This comic runs the same way: the whole pipeline that picks the news, writes the script, and renders the panels sits on our own racks, and the quality call belongs to a deterministic gate rather than a model claiming it did fine.
