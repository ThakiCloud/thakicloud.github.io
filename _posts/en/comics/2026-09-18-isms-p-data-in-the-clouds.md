## What this means for ThakiCloud

What ThakiCloud reads out of the audit-tightening news is the address of your personal data. In an era of stricter audits, the question that matters more is not 'do you have a security program' but 'where does the data actually live, and who controls it.' On-prem gives the most honest answer. The moment your data runs inside your own facility, the inspector opens the door and you point right at it: 'right here.' You stop handing your information to someone else's cloud, with its expiration dates and price lists. Paxis assembles the audit work into agents; Metis puts the training and inference platform inside the same walls, so the 'information security management system' is run from the inside rather than rented from the outside. We'd rather own the address than chase renewals.
