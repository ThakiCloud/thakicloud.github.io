## What this means for ThakiCloud

The strip pokes at "final exam first." But the same question applies to any AI education: whose machine is the student on? On a public platform, the syllabus ends up being "how to use our model," and the meter starts at lesson one.

ThakiCloud runs the other way. Paxis agents learn on your own hardware, and Metis trains and serves on your own data. It is not that schools do not matter. It is that the school name matters. Graduate from your own school and the diploma, the model, stays in your hands. Tuition is just the power bill.
