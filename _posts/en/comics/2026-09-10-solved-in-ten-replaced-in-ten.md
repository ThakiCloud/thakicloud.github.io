## What this means for ThakiCloud

The ten-minute fix for a five-year problem takes two parts. An agent platform that scales the swarm and reads every record, and an inference engine that binds the accumulated data into one cause. When Paxis and Metis click like that, the problem's age stops mattering. The bigger question is where the agents run. In the comic, the agent that found the test server also switched off production. That is why ThakiCloud runs its agent orchestration on-prem. Agents inside your own building mean the mistakes are ones you pick up yourself, and the data never leaves the walls. The AX survival playbook, in the end, is less about how fast things get solved and more about who owns the agents and the data.
