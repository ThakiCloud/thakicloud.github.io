## What this means for ThakiCloud

It doesn't matter whether the typhoon reads 106 or 1,006. The trouble starts the moment you paddle out onto it. Every time Paxis shouts for more agents, GPU usage climbs — and that usage prints itself as somebody else's revenue. ThakiCloud's answer is boring on purpose: keep the models, the data and the GPUs inside your own walls, and the storm stays a headline. Metis runs inference on your servers, Paxis orchestrates the agents, and the only number that lands on the bill is the electric one. This strip was made that way, too. The surfing, however, is ongoing.
