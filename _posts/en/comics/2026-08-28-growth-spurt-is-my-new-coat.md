## What this means for ThakiCloud

The 133-trillion story comes down to one thing: who holds the card. Rent it, and the chipmaker's growth prints straight onto your invoice. Own the hardware and run it in your own facility, and that same growth becomes your asset, with only the electric bill left over. That is the shape ThakiCloud runs in. Paxis orchestrates the agents, Metis handles training and inference, and the models, data and GPUs all stay inside your walls. This very comic is made on that setup, from news selection to the final frame. The growth spurt never reaches the window.
