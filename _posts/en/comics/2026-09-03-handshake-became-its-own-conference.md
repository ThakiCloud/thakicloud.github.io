## What this means for ThakiCloud

When the conference hall is bigger than the server room, something is structurally off. The AI-transformation stage is all handshake and banner, while the one question that actually matters, where the AI runs and whose building it lives in, rarely makes the agenda. ThakiCloud's answer is to stop renting stages and start moving the server room in. Paxis orchestration and Metis inference run inside your own building, and the managed-service part ends at your front door. Keys and plugs stay in your pocket, and the remote control needs no conference.
