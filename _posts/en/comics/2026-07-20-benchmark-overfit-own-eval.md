The talk of the week: a Chinese open model posted a top-tier score on an internal cybersecurity eval. Almost immediately, the timeline pushed back, with chatter that Moonshot may have benchmark-overfit. Benchmark-overfitting is when a model memorizes the shape of the test: great scores, shaky on problems it hasn't seen. An eval, meanwhile, is just the exam that measures whether a model can actually do the work. This strip pokes at the gap between a leaderboard rank and whether the thing works on your own job.
