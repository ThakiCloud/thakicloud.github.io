## What this means for ThakiCloud

Resale revenue looks healthy right up until you notice the margin is set by someone else's price list. That is why ThakiCloud puts Paxis at the center: automate the customer's actual work with agents, and the unit you charge for becomes work completed, not instance-hours rented. Metis owns the inference bill those agents run up, and for customers whose data and models cannot leave the building, Aegis puts the whole thing inside their own facility. A lifeline you plant outlasts one you lease.
