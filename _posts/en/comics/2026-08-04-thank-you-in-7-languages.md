This short video teaches thank you in Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, French, Russian and Arabic. Each word is spoken twice, slowly, in a native voice. Watch it with a child and repeat each word out loud once. By the end, a child can say thank you in seven languages.

**Animated edition - hear today's phrase in each language**

Every word is spoken twice by a native voice, with English subtitles.

**When you use it**

- **Korean**: In Korean, thank you is gomapseumnida.
- **English**: In English, we simply say thank you.
- **Japanese**: In Japanese, thank you is arigato.
- **Chinese**: In Chinese, thank you is xiexie.
- **Francais**: In French, thank you is merci.
- **Russian**: In Russian, thank you is spasibo.
- **Arabic**: In Arabic, thank you is shukran.

## What this means for ThakiCloud

Localising educational content usually multiplies cost by the number of languages. Here the scenes are produced once, with only the voice, subtitles and on-screen text changing per language, so an eighth language costs almost nothing. The same logic applies internally, where the multilingual speech model's location matters. Fine-tuning and serving it on Metis keeps training data and children's voices inside the company cluster. For organisations whose data cannot leave the building, that on-premise setup makes real sovereignty possible.
