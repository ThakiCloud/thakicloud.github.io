## What this means for ThakiCloud

The bidding war is really about how many years of rent you sign for, not whether you can build. Every data center that goes up is another meter turning, and sovereignty is decided by the scope of control, not the size of the lot. ThakiCloud does not enter the bid. Paxis's agent orchestration and Metis's training and inference run inside the customer's own racks, the closet at home, so to speak, and Taki just guards the nameplate on that rack. The zero was never a loss. It was arithmetic: if the building is yours, the rent is nothing.
