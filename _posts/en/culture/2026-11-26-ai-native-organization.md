*AI-Native Culture 08*

## Does Buying AI Make a Company an AI Company?

Giving every employee a generative AI account can be a good start. But if the existing approval system, information structure, and work flow stay the same, the effect may remain in individual document writing and information search. Adopting AI and designing the organization on the assumption of AI are different problems.

The AI-Native Organization we talk about is not one that forces AI into every task. It is one that separates the judgments humans should make from the work systems should execute, safely connects organizational knowledge with tools, measures outcomes, and improves continuously.

## Directions Visible at Global Technology Companies

Google DORA's 2025 research presents the view that to raise the value of AI investment, the organization's foundational systems deserve more attention than the tools.[1] Microsoft's 2026 Work Trend Index presents the themes of expanding human agency, redesigning work, and organizational learning.[2]

In his 2025 CoreAI-related message, Satya Nadella described the direction of agentic applications with memory and permissions.[3] Shopify formally introduced expectations for AI use by all employees along with tool access and a culture of sharing experiments.[4] These are cases from companies in different businesses, not statistics representing the state of Silicon Valley as a whole.

## The Foundations of Organizational Capability

| Foundation | Meaning |
|---|---|
| AI Infrastructure | Model access, computing, cost and availability management |
| Enterprise Knowledge | Organizational knowledge with permissions and provenance |
| Agentic Workflow | Execution that connects work steps with tools |
| Verification | Quality assessment, testing, review and approval |
| Governance | Data security, responsibility, traceability and stop |
| Learning Culture | Records of success and failure, education and improvement |

If an organization has AI infrastructure but no access to its knowledge, models find it hard to understand the organization's situation. If knowledge is connected but there is no action authority and no verification, real work automation is constrained. The technical foundation and the work culture are not separate challenges.

## Adoption Starts With Small Work

The first step is observing current work to identify repetitive cost, errors, and waiting time. The second is choosing low-risk, measurable work and experimenting in an approved AI environment. The third is connecting the capability to the work system once it meets verification and operating criteria. Finally, improve or stop it based on real cost, quality, and user feedback.

For example, if improving customer support document search, the goal is not simply to increase the number of answers generated. You must check whether responses were based on correct sources, whether time to resolution and repeat questions decreased, and whether inaccessible customer information was exposed. Model cost and review cost must also be subtracted from the time saved.

The NIST Generative AI Profile can serve as a reference for considering risk and reliability at the organizational level.[5] Risk management should become part of work design, not a separate step that stops innovation.

## Organizational Culture Ultimately Decides Competitiveness

Hiring must evaluate foundational expertise and AI collaboration together. Managers must redesign work, and knowledge must not stay with individuals alone. Agents execute within the necessary scope, engineers take responsibility for quality, the organization controls risk, and it gives juniors opportunities to grow.

This is the connection point of the AI-Native culture this series has discussed. As a company that builds AI infrastructure, platforms, and agent technology, ThakiCloud is interested in the technology that makes this kind of environment possible. The principles proposed here are our aspirations, not a declaration that every system has already been completed.

A company's competitiveness in the AI era does not come from using the most AI. It comes from an organization where people and technology produce better results together, verify those results, and accumulate the experience as the foundation for the next challenge.
